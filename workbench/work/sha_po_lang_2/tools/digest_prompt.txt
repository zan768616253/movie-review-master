# 《杀破狼2》剧情简介与人物

## 剧情简介

《杀破狼2》是一部香港与中国合拍的动作犯罪片，也是2005年《杀破狼》的主题性续作，但故事彼此独立。

影片讲述香港卧底警察**陈志杰（Kit）**，潜入一个庞大的犯罪集团执行任务。不料行动中身份暴露，在一次失败的抓捕中被绑架，并被伪造身份送往泰国一所黑暗监狱。

与此同时，泰国狱警**猜猜（Chatchai）**正为患有白血病的女儿奔走。他急需找到骨髓配型者来挽救女儿的生命。而命运的安排下，新被关押的囚犯陈志杰，正是全球唯一与他女儿匹配的供体。

这座监狱表面正常，实际上由冷酷优雅的典狱长**高晋（Ko Chun）**掌控，他暗中经营着一个庞大的器官贩卖网络。幕后主使**洪文刚（Hung Mun-Gong）**因心脏衰竭濒临死亡，不惜一切代价甚至牺牲亲人来获取新心脏。

另一方面，陈志杰的上司兼叔叔**陈国华**不顾上级命令，远赴泰国追查真相并试图营救侄子。

随着监狱黑幕逐渐浮出水面，猜猜面临艰难抉择：是继续服从腐败体制保住工作，还是帮助陈志杰逃出生天。在生死关头，语言与国界不再重要，两人最终联手，对抗高晋与犯罪集团，展开一场震撼的终极对决——不仅为生存而战，也为守护家人而战。

* 犯罪集团幕后主脑
* 心脏衰竭，生命垂危
* 极端残忍，为生存不择手段

# Output Format: Plot Digest for 杀破狼2 (SPL II: A Time for Consequences)
Write the entire digest in Chinese. Be detailed — the reviewer cannot watch the movie, so your digest is their only source.

## 角色表 (Character Table)
For each important character (max 8-10), provide:
- 原名: Original name from the movie
- 身份: Role (protagonist / antagonist / ally / victim / etc.)
- 关系: Key relationships to other characters
- 动机: What they want
- 结局: What ultimately happens to them
- 性格特点: 1-2 defining personality traits

## 权力结构 (Power Map)
Describe who controls what, who deceives whom, and how power shifts throughout the movie. Who is pretending? Who is trapped? Who holds the real leverage?

## 剧情脉络 (Plot Beats)
List 30-50 major story beats in chronological order. For EACH beat:
- 事件: What happens (2-3 sentences, vivid and specific)
- 因果: Why this happens / what it causes (the causal chain)
- 台词: Key dialogue if any (quote the most impactful lines verbatim)
- 情绪: Emotional register (tension / humor / horror / tenderness / etc.)

IMPORTANT: Be detailed enough that someone who has never seen the movie can retell the FULL story. Each major scene transition should be a separate beat. Do NOT merge multiple scenes into one vague beat.

## 名场面 (Reviewable Moments)
List 10-15 moments a movie reviewer would love to describe:
- Visually absurd or striking images
- Ironic situations or logic failures
- Satisfying revenge or comeuppance
- Embarrassing or humiliating scenes
- Shocking twists or reveals
- Action sequences with interesting choreography
- Emotional gut-punches
- Unintentionally funny moments

For each moment, include 2-4 sentences of vivid detail — enough for the reviewer to narrate it as if they watched it.

## 核心矛盾 (Core Conflict & Themes)
- What is this movie really about? (1-2 sentences)
- What is the central irony or contradiction?
- What question does the movie leave the audience with?

## 结局 (Full Ending)
Describe the ending in FULL detail:
- How the climax resolves
- Every main character's final fate
- The emotional aftertaste
- Any post-credits scene or final twist
